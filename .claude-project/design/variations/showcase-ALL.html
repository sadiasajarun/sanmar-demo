The three directions

All three carry identical content, identical information architecture and identical colour semantics. What differs is density, type, depth and how much of Sanmar's own brand language the software speaks.

ASanmar Trust

The client's brand extended into software

Warm paper ground, deep evergreen structure, restrained brass accents. A high-contrast serif carries the score numeral and page titles against a plain humanist sans. Medium density, 10px corners, low warm shadows. Reads as an established developer's institutional tool.

BOperations Console

The tool the floor lives in

Near-black navigation against a cool light workspace, with one indigo accent used sparingly enough that it always means "acted upon". Every numeral is monospace and tabular so columns align down a 200-row table. 4px corners. No shadows anywhere — borders do all the structural work.

Fixed across all three

Score-band semantics do not change with the visual direction. Choosing a direction never changes what a colour means — and red is reserved exclusively for things a human must fix, never for a bad lead.

Hot · 75–100Green is money and "go". Qualified, route immediately, 2-hour first-contact SLA.
Warm · 55–74Positive but not urgent. Qualified, normal round-robin, 24-hour SLA.
Nurture · 35–54Cool and later. No agent assigned; enters the automated email sequence.
Cold / Garbage · 0–34Deliberately unalarming grey. Goes to the Review Queue with a reason, never deleted.
Red · reservedSLA breach, failed automation, over-capacity agent, dead-letter webhook. Never lead quality.

What happens after you choose

Selecting a direction unlocks generation of the full eleven-page prototype in that visual language.

  1. Compare the two screens above in all three directions — the Lead Detail screen is the one that has to carry the demo, so weight it heaviest.
  2. Record the choice in .claude-project/status/mysanmar/DESIGN_STATUS.md: set selected_variation: A | B | C and approved: true. A mix is fine — for example B's density with C's score ring; note it in the same file.
  3. Re-run /fullstack-pm mysanmar --phase P3-design --resume --path D:\mysanmar-demo.
  4. All eleven pages get generated into design/html/manager/ (7) and design/html/agent/ (4), then pass design QA and the role-folder gate.

Two things still need the client before this goes into a meeting: Sanmar's real project names and price bands (sample data currently uses Ocean City plus illustrative siblings), and the name of the call-analysis product they use or intend to buy — the summary card is built against a generic signed webhook until then.
